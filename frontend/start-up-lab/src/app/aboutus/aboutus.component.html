Our Vision

We aim to revolutionize the way companies recruit talent by introducing innovative solutions that make the process efficient and insightful.

Our Services

  • Recruitment through engaging games designed to reduce pre-interview anxiety.
  • Personality trait analysis based on gameplay data.
  • Insights into candidates' strengths and weaknesses for better hiring decisions.

Why Choose Us?

Our service is tailored for innovative startups and medium-sized high-tech companies. We help recruiters and interviewers identify the best talent while ensuring a positive experience for candidates.

How It Works

  1. Candidates play a short game 10 minutes before their interview.
  2. The gameplay is analyzed to extract valuable insights.
  3. Recruiters receive a detailed report to guide their interview process.
